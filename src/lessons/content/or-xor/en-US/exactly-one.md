# Challenge: exactly one, without XOR

- **Your task:** light **Y** when **exactly one** of A and B is on – but there is no XOR gate this time.
- Predict-first: the light stays hidden until you press **Check**, so reason it out for all 4 rows.
- Think of it as "at least one" **and** "not both".

<!-- hints -->

- "At least one" is A OR B. "Not both" is NOT (A AND B).
- Y is on when both of those are true: combine them with one more AND gate.
