# NOT from NAND

- From now on you may use **only NAND gates**. Engineers like NAND: it's cheap to make, and it can build every other gate.
- **Your task:** Y = NOT A, using one NAND gate.

<!-- hints -->

- A NAND gate has two inputs, and you have one switch. A switch's output can go to more than one place.
- Connect A to **both** inputs of the NAND: A NAND A = NOT (A AND A) = NOT A.
