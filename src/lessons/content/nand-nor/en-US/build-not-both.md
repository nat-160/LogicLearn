# Build "not both"

- **Your task:** light **Y** unless **both** A and B are on.
- There is no NAND gate in the parts list: build it from the gates you know.
- Try all four rows – especially the one where both switches are off.

<!-- hints -->

- "Not both" is the opposite of "both". Which gate means "both"?
- Put a NOT after an AND: Y = NOT (A AND B).
