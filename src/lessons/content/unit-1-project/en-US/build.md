# Design and build

- **1.** Choose a brief.
- **2.** Fill in the open rows of **Your rule** (on the right).
- **3.** Build the circuit with the gates you know.
- **4.** Press **Check my project**. Every line of the checklist should say **Done**.

<!-- hints -->

- Read your rule one row at a time: for which rows must the light be on?
- Look for a pattern you know: does a row need two things at once (AND), or either one (OR)?
