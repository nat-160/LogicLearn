# Door alarm

- **D** is on when the door is open. **R** is on when the alarm is set. **P** is a panic button – it's on only while you hold it down.
- The red light **L** is the alarm.
- When the alarm is set, opening the door or pressing panic sounds the alarm. When it's not set, opening the door doesn't.
- **You decide:** does the panic button work when the alarm is not set?

<!-- hints -->

- Read your rule one row at a time: for which rows must the light be on?
- Start with the rows where the alarm is set (R = 1). Which of them sound the alarm?
- "Door open **and** alarm set" is one way to sound the alarm. The panic button is another – join the two ways with OR.
- If panic always works, L = P OR (D AND R).
