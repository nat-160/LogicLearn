# What is a logic gate?

- Computers work with **signals** that are either **on (1)** or **off (0)**.
- A **logic gate** takes one or more input signals and produces an output signal by following a simple rule.
- In this lesson:
  - **switches** are inputs (tap one to turn it on or off)
  - **lights** are outputs
- **Try it:** the example below has one switch, one **NOT gate** and one light.
  - Flip switch **A**. What does the light do?
- The **NOT gate** outputs the _opposite_ of its input:

| A   | NOT A |
| --- | ----- |
| 0   | 1     |
| 1   | 0     |
