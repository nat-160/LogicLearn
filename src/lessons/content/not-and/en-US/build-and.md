# Build it: AND

- **Your task:** light **Y** only when switches **A** and **B** are **both** on.
- Use the truth table to test **every** row, not just the ones you expect to work.
  - Start with the row where everything is **0**: it is the one most often skipped.
- Press **Check** when every row looks right.

<!-- hints -->

- Which gate is on only when **all** of its inputs are on?
- Add an AND gate, then wire **A** and **B** to its two inputs and its output to **Y**.
