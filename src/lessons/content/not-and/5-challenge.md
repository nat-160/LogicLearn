## Challenge: on, but not that one

- **Your task:** light **Y** when switch **A** is on **and** switch **B** is **off**.
- This is a **predict-first** challenge: the light won't show its value until you press **Check**.
  - Work it out in your head, or on paper, for all 4 rows first.
- Hint: you will need more than one kind of gate.
