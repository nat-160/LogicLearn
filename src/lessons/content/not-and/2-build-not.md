## Build it: NOT

- Switch **A** and light **Y** are already placed for you (they are locked).
- **Your task:** make light **Y** show the _opposite_ of switch **A**.
- How:
  1. Add a **NOT** gate from the parts list (tap it, or drag it onto the canvas).
  2. Connect **A** to the NOT gate's input, then its output to **Y**. Tap one pin, then tap the other.
  3. Flip **A** both ways, then press **Check**.
