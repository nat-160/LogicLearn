# Your project

- Clocks, microwaves and calculators show numbers with **seven segments**, named **a** to **g**:
  - **a** top, **b** top right, **c** bottom right, **d** bottom, **e** bottom left, **f** top left, **g** middle.
- Each segment is a light with its own input pin. A digit is a pattern of lit segments: **1** is just b and c.
- In this project, two switches A and B make a number from 0 to 3 in binary (A is the twos bit, B the ones bit), and the display shows it.
