# Predict a truth table

- The circuit's light is hidden. **Your task:** fill in what **Y** will be for every row, then press **Check my predictions**.
- Work from left to right: first what the NOT gate outputs, then the AND gate.
- You can flip the switches to move the highlighted row, but the light won't tell you the answer.

<!-- hints -->

- Start with the all-zeros row: A = 0, so NOT A = 1. What does AND give with B = 0?
- Y is 1 only when NOT A **and** B are both 1 – that is, when A is 0 and B is 1.
