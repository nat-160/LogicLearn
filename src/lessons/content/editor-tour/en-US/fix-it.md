# Fix a mistake

- Someone put a **NOT** gate between A and Y, so Y does the opposite of A.
- **Your task:** make Y show A again, with no gates in between.
- **Delete:** tap a part or wire to select it, then press **Delete** in the toolbar (or the Delete key).
- **Undo / Redo** (↶ ↷) take back a change if you make a mistake.

<!-- hints -->

- Tap the NOT gate to select it, then press **Delete** in the toolbar. Its wires go too.
- Now connect A's dot (right side) straight to Y's dot (left side).
- Deleted the wrong thing? Press **Undo** (↶), or **Start over**.
