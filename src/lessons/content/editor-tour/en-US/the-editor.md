# Welcome to the editor

- The **canvas** in the middle is where you build circuits.
  - **Switches** (left) are inputs: tap one to turn it on (1) or off (0).
  - **Lights** (right) are outputs: they light up when they get a 1.
- Try it: flip switch **A** in the example and watch light **Y**.
- The **truth table** (right) lists every combination of the switches. The highlighted row is the one the switches are set to now.
