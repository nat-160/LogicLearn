# Add parts and connect them

- **Your task:** add a switch and a light, and connect them so the light shows the switch.
- **Add a part:** tap it in the parts list (or drag it onto the canvas).
- **Connect:** tap the switch's dot (its output, on the right), then the light's dot (its input, on the left).
- Flip the switch to test it, then press **Check**.

<!-- hints -->

- Tap **Switch** in the parts list, then tap **Light**. They are labelled A and Y.
- Tap the small dot on the right of switch A, then the dot on the left of light Y. A wire appears.
- Flip A on and off: Y should follow it. Then press **Check**.
