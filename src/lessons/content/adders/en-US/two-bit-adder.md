# Challenge: a 2-bit adder

- Add two 2-bit numbers: **A1 A0** plus **B1 B0** (A0 and B0 are the ones bits).
- The answer has three bits: **S2 S1 S0** (S2 is the final carry).
- Predict-first: the lights stay hidden until you press **Check**.

<!-- hints -->

- Bit 0 is a half adder. Bit 1 is a full adder whose carry in is bit 0's carry; its carry out is S2.
