# On and off, 1 and 0

- Computers work with **signals** that are either **on** or **off**.
  - We write on as **1** and off as **0**. There is nothing in between.
- A **switch** is an **input**: you decide whether it sends 1 or 0.
- A **light** is an **output**: it shows the signal it receives.
- Try it: flip switch **A** and watch light **Y**. The thick green wire carries a 1; the thin dark wire carries a 0.
