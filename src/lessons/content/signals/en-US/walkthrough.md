# How signals travel

A circuit passes signals along wires. Watch switch **A**, its wire, and the two lights.

<!-- frames -->

- Switch **A** is off, so it sends **0** along its wire. Light **Y** reads that 0 and stays off.
- Turn **A** on: it now sends **1**. The wire carries the 1 to **Y**, so **Y** lights up. The wire looks thicker when it carries a 1.
- Light **Z** has no wire. Nothing tells it 0 or 1, so it shows **?** – unknown (X). That's not "off": it means "not connected".
- So every signal is **0** or **1**, and a part with nothing connected reads **X**. When you see a **?**, look for a missing wire.
