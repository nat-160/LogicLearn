# Challenge: one switch, two lights

- **Your task:** make **both** lights Y and Z show switch **A**.
- One output can feed **many** inputs – a wire can split.
- This is a **predict-first** challenge: the lights won't show their values until you press **Check**.

<!-- hints -->

- Connect A to Y, then connect A to Z as well: tap A's dot again, then Z's dot.
