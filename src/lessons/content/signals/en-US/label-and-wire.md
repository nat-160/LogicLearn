# Label and wire

- Every input and output has a **label**, like A or Y. The truth table and the checker find parts by their label.
- **Your task:**
  1. Rename light **L1** to **Y** and light **L2** to **Z** (select a light, then type in the **Label** box on the right).
  2. Connect switch **A** to light **Y**, and switch **B** to light **Z**.
- Flip both switches to test every row, then press **Check**.

<!-- hints -->

- Tap light L1 to select it. The panel on the right shows its **Label**: change it to Y. Do the same for L2 → Z.
- Connect each switch's dot (right side) to its light's dot (left side): A to Y, B to Z.
- Check the truth table: Y should match A, and Z should match B, in every row.
