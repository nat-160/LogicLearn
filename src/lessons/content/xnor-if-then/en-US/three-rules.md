# Challenge: three rules

- Three rules (the author will add the story):
  - Rule 1: **if A then C**.
  - Rule 2: **B and C match**.
  - Rule 3: **not both A and B**.
- **Your task:** light **Y** when all three rules are kept.
- Predict-first: the light stays hidden until you press **Check**.

<!-- hints -->

- Translate one rule at a time into a gate (or two), then AND all the rules together.
